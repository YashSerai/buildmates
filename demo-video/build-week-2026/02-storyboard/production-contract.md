# Production contract

## Master

- 1920×1080, 30 fps, maximum 3:00.
- Keep primary text and product evidence inside a centered safe region suitable for a later vertical crop.
- Use the Buildmates visual world: warm paper, deep moss, sage, and controlled orange accents.
- Do not burn in full subtitles. Use short audience-facing labels only when the narration does not make the product action clear. Export an SRT separately.

## Persistent transition object

`NETWORKING → WORK → living profile → ranked builder node → mutual connection line → room → Circle → city bubble → topic bubble → Buildmates mark`

The shared object owns each boundary. Text collapses into a shape before travel. Default transition travel is 12–16 frames, adjusted only when readability or product footage requires it.

## Iterations per scene

1. Story clarity: the action and proof are understandable without decorative motion.
2. Art direction: composition, typography, color, and depth belong to Buildmates.
3. Motion integration: entrance, exit, transition ownership, sound cue, and safe crop are complete.

## Visual gates

- No clipped or overlapping text.
- No low-contrast text, white-on-white states, unintended dead space, or template-looking filler.
- Product controls shown in footage must be real and working.
- Generated profile, room, and Circle pages must be checked at desktop and phone sizes, including the lower page.
- Motion cannot be required to understand the scene and must have a reduced-motion-safe product state.
- Each selected scene must have a still contact sheet and a short playback review note.
