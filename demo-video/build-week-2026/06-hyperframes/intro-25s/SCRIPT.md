# Buildmates intro - 25 seconds

## Voiceover

At the center of networking is work.

Work gives builders a real reason to talk. Maybe they are both trying to make voice agents feel less robotic. Maybe one is building the thing the other has been thinking about for weeks.

Nobody has time to keep a profile current, post about every small thing they ship, then explain each project again in a cold message.

There should be a better way to share what you are working on.

So why not let the platform you already use to build keep track of what you are building?

Introducing Buildmates.

## Visual timing

- 0.0-3.5: NETWORKING arrives as a moving typographic reel. NET and ING fall away. WORK remains inside the orange field.
- 3.5-8.2: WORK becomes a bridge between two voice-agent experiments. Their waveforms become more natural as the builders' work overlaps.
- 8.2-13.7: Project updates, profile edits, posts, and cold-message explanations pile up around a builder.
- 13.7-16.3: The pile collapses into one calm, living profile.
- 16.3-22.0: Work from active projects flows through Codex into the profile without another manual update loop.
- 22.0-25.0: The same three paths resolve into the Buildmates mark and wordmark, held for a clean cut to the live landing page.
