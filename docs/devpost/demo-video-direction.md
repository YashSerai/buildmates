# Buildmates demo video direction

The Buildmates video follows the narrative method used for Soulspace: begin with a concise product thesis expressed through choreographed motion, reverse the old assumption, then prove the new model with the real product. It is not a screen-recording montage.

## Story shape

1. **The old network.** Static resumes, stale profiles, follower counts, and cold outreach form a crowded professional graph while the work people are doing changes underneath it.
2. **The missing context.** Codex already understands a builder's current projects, interests, and working style, but that context normally ends inside one person's tasks.
3. **The reversal.** Networking should begin with current work. Buildmates turns only user-approved summaries into a living builder profile and a private matching signal.
4. **The product proof.** Show real onboarding, generated profile direction and publication, ranked reciprocal introductions, mutual approval, a work-themed room, Work Pulse, the anonymous Map, and the Build Graph.
5. **The outcome.** End on the persistent relationship layer: a useful conversation becomes a Connection and can grow into a Circle without exposing raw Codex context.

The final video must remain under three minutes, be public or unlisted on YouTube, and include audible narration explaining what was built, how Codex accelerated the implementation, and where GPT-5.6 was used.

## Visual production contract

- Use purpose-built kinetic scenes for the thesis and transition; use real production capture for product claims.
- HyperFrames is suitable for the HTML/CSS/JS-driven opening and transitions. Remotion is suitable for the timed master composition, typography, captured product footage, and final render.
- Cut loading, typing, OAuth waiting, and repeated navigation. Never fabricate a product response.
- The `qa_visual_*` and `demo_network_*` records are demo fixtures. They may appear in the video, but their counts must not be presented as organic adoption.
- Capture desktop product footage at a stable readable scale, with selective phone footage only where responsiveness or generated-profile reflow is part of the claim.

## Cleanup

Keep the demo fixtures through judging. Remove them after the winners announcement on August 12, 2026, or before publishing any organic traction claim, whichever happens first. Exact cleanup SQL lives beside each fixture under `scripts/fixtures/`.
